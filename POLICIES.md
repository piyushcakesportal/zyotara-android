# Draft Policies for DashaVaani

These drafts must be reviewed and published on a public website before Play Store or App Store submission.

## Privacy summary

DashaVaani collects a display name, date of birth, time of birth, place of birth and the question the user chooses to ask. These details are used only to generate and save the requested reading.

The test version stores data locally on the user's device. When live astrology and AI services are connected, the final privacy policy must name or describe the categories of service providers receiving birth data and explain retention, deletion and security practices.

Users can delete individual readings, all prediction history, or all locally stored personal data from Settings.

DashaVaani should not request phone contacts, precise current location, photos or advertising identifiers for its core prediction feature.

## Astrology disclaimer

DashaVaani provides astrology-based guidance for reflection and entertainment. Astrology is not scientifically established as a method for predicting outcomes. Readings are interpretations, not guarantees.

Users must not rely on DashaVaani as a substitute for medical, mental-health, legal, financial, emergency or professional advice. Users remain responsible for their own decisions.

The app does not provide predictions about death, serious illness, pregnancy outcome, a child's sex, court-case certainty, betting, lottery results, harm, revenge, surveillance or another person's private behaviour.

## Terms summary

- Users must provide birth details they are authorised to use.
- Users must not submit another person's sensitive personal information without consent.
- The service may limit free questions to manage costs and prevent abuse.
- Generated content may contain errors or incomplete interpretations.
- The service may refuse unsafe or unsupported questions.
- Users may delete their locally stored data at any time.
